SHAFT Support

For the SHAFT apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Contact

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL to report a problem or ask a question. Say which device and app version you use (Settings → App → Version) and what you expected to happen. For a security problem, put "Security" in the subject.

SHAFT is a one-person project: questions are answered as time allows.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 26 or later running SHAFT. The SHAFT app for Mac installs and sets it up.
  • Tailscale on the Mac and on your iPhone or iPad, signed in to the same tailnet.
  • A model provider set up on the Mac (for example an OpenRouter API key or a ChatGPT plan).

Just looking? Tap Try the demo on the app's first screen. It runs on sample data and needs none of the above.

Pair your iPhone or iPad

  1. On the Mac, open the SHAFT app → Settings → Use SHAFT from iPhone/iPad, or run shaft web pair --name iphone-app in a terminal. Either gives you a pairing link that works once, for 10 minutes.
  2. Copy the link. Universal Clipboard carries it to your iPhone or iPad.
  3. In the app, tap Paste, then Pair.

The Apple Watch app needs no pairing of its own: it works through the paired iPhone.

Common problems

The app cannot reach your Mac
Check that Tailscale is connected on this device and on the Mac, and that the Mac is awake and SHAFT is running.
"This device was unpaired on the Mac"
The pairing was revoked (or SHAFT was reinstalled). Make a new link on the Mac and pair again.
No notifications
Push notifications are not yet available for self-hosted servers. New messages appear while the app is open.
Voice mode does not start
Allow the microphone and speech recognition for SHAFT in Settings → Privacy & Security. The first use may download the speech model for your language.
The app says the server is too old
Update SHAFT on the Mac (SHAFT app → Settings → Services → Update server).

Privacy

The app sends your data only to the SHAFT server on your own Mac. Read the privacy policy.
